# TapNSwitch licensing

TapNSwitch is **free software** under the [GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](LICENSE) (AGPLv3).

## What AGPLv3 gives you (and what we want to keep)

- **Venue use:** Run and modify the software to score and operate physical axe-throwing venues you control.
- **Copyleft on modifications:** If you **distribute** the software or a modified version (binaries, packages, or source to third parties), those derivatives must remain under AGPLv3. You cannot distribute proprietary forks or closed-source re-wraps built on this code.
- **Network copyleft (SaaS):** If you run a modified version as a **network service** that users interact with over a network, you must offer corresponding source to those users under AGPLv3. This closes the common GPL loophole where hosted scoring platforms never distribute binaries.
- **Source access:** Anyone who receives a distributed copy, or uses the software over a network under AGPLv3’s terms, is entitled to the corresponding source.

That copyleft is intentional: changes that reach others—whether as downloads or as a hosted service—stay open. You cannot monetize a closed fork or closed hosted fork of TapNSwitch.

## What requires a commercial license

AGPLv3 still permits some commercial activity when source obligations are met. We use a **dual-licensing** model for uses that compete with our business or re-package the product for profit without meeting those obligations:

Contact [contact@yeetbox.net](mailto:contact@yeetbox.net) for a **separate written commercial license** before:

- Selling, sublicensing, or white-labeling TapNSwitch (or a derivative) as a scoring or venue-management product
- Offering TapNSwitch (or a derivative) as a **hosted SaaS or multi-tenant platform** to third-party venues for a fee **without** providing source under AGPLv3
- Deploying a **federation-wide or franchise-wide platform** on behalf of a sport governing body or network (including the International Axe Throwing Federation (IATF) as an organization)

**Individual venues**—including IATF-member locations—may use and modify TapNSwitch under AGPLv3 for their own sites without a commercial license, provided they comply with AGPLv3 when distributing software or offering network access to others.

## Relationship to IATF

We support axe venues operating in line with community and sport standards, including IATF guidelines where they apply to play at a venue. This policy does **not** grant IATF or similar bodies the right to ship or sell TapNSwitch as their own platform without a commercial agreement.
